## Common Mistakes When Calculating Automation ROI

Many businesses overestimate savings by ignoring hidden costs or underestimating implementation time. Here are pitfalls to avoid:

- **Forgetting maintenance**: Automation needs updates and monitoring. Budget 10 to 15% of the initial cost annually, plus software subscriptions, usually a few tens of euros a month for an SME workload.
- **Ignoring training time**: Employees need time to learn new workflows. Include this in your payback period.
- **Overlooking integration costs**: Connecting automation to existing software may require extra development. Count it in the implementation cost.
- **Assuming 100% adoption**: Not every task will be automated perfectly. Use a realistic adoption rate (e.g., 80%).

Our calculator includes fields for these factors, so your estimate stays grounded.

## Real-World Example: Invoice Processing

Let's say your team processes 500 invoices per month. Each invoice takes 10 minutes by hand, and your loaded labor cost is €25 an hour: that's 83 hours a month on this task, with a 5% error rate on top. Automation could cut processing time to 2 minutes per invoice, the time a person still spends checking it.

If every invoice went through the automation, you would save 67 hours a month, or €20,000 a year in labor. At a realistic 80% adoption, it's 53 hours a month and €16,000 a year, plus €1,200 a year of corrections avoided if the error rate drops from 5% to 1% and each correction takes 15 minutes.

Now the costs. Assume €5,000 to build and connect the workflow, 8 hours of training, €30 a month of software and 15% of the build cost a year for maintenance. The net saving is €16,090 a year, the payback period is just under four months, and the first-year ROI is 209%. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is the ROI calculator free to use?

Yes, the calculator is free. You can use it as many times as you like and save reports as PDFs without paying anything or leaving an email.

### What if I don't know my error rate?

Use the default value provided. It comes from our invoice example, 5% by hand and 1% once automated, not from a published industry average. Better: count the corrections your team made over the last two weeks and divide by the number of runs. You can adjust it later if you have more precise data.

### Can I use this calculator for any type of process?

The calculator works best for repetitive, rule-based processes like data entry, document handling, and routine approvals. For complex, judgment-heavy tasks, the estimates may be less accurate.

### How accurate are the ROI estimates?

The estimates are based on standard formulas and your inputs. They are meant to be a starting point for your analysis, not a guarantee. Actual results will vary depending on implementation quality and other factors. The two inputs that move the result most are the minutes saved per run and the adoption rate: measure them on a pilot before a full rollout.
