The problem is almost never the technology

Most of the leaders we meet have already tried a conversational assistant. Some even launched a pilot that never got past the pilot stage. The blocker is rarely technical: it comes from AI being dropped onto a process nobody had mapped, without knowing what gain was sought or how it would be measured.

Our position is simple and it sometimes costs us contracts: we refuse to automate a process the client cannot describe. A badly designed process you automate becomes a badly designed process that produces its errors faster, at a larger scale, and with added confidence. That is what the diagnosis is for.

Frequently asked questions

From what company size is this relevant?

Our clients range from independents to 500-person companies, with a core target between 20 and 250 employees. Below 20, AI is still useful but a full engagement is rarely justified beyond training and two or three automations. Above 500, organizational complexity becomes the real subject, and that is precisely the terrain Rémy Ginoux practiced for 25 years in industrial groups.

How fast do you see results?

The diagnosis delivers a quantified picture in 1 to 3 weeks. A first use case goes from Proof of Concept to MVP in production in 13 days on average. Concretely, a company starting in January generally has a first automation running for good before the end of the first quarter, which is the frame in which we talk about productive time gains.

How much does it cost, in an honest range?

Diagnosis between €3,000 and €5,000, AI Sprint from PoC to MVP between €8,000 and €15,000, monthly co-piloting between €1,500 and €4,000 (excl. VAT). These ranges are displayed on the homepage, not reserved for a quote. The final price depends on scope, never on your revenue.

Do you need clean data before starting?

No, and it is rarely the case. The real state of your data is part of what the diagnosis measures. Many projects start by structuring what sits in emails and shared folders, because that is the condition for AI to produce anything beyond the generic.

Will our teams have to relearn everything?

No. We plug automations into the tools already in place rather than imposing one more, and we train on what actually changes, not on theory. Adoption work is part of the engagement when useful, as in our freelancer workshop.
