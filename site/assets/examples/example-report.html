🏢 Phase ①: Project & Technology Moat
Who is Soitec?

Soitec (EURONEXT: SOI) is a European leader in innovative semiconductor substrate technology, specialized in SOI (Silicon-On-Insulator) substrates. The company designs and manufactures high-performance wafers for AI, edge computing and high-frequency applications (5G, RF).

Key figures: FY2025 revenue ~€680M · Operating EBITDA ~€45M · Cash €180M · Headcount ~2,100 employees (France/Asia/US).

Moat & Positioning
Proprietary technology: SOI patents and unique manufacturing processes (Smart Cut®, hybrid bonding). Very high barrier to entry.
Tier-1 customers: ST Microelectronics (30% of revenue), Samsung (15%), Intel, AMD, TSMC. Structural partnership relationships.
Undisputed leadership: Near-monopoly on high-performance SOI substrates (95% of the addressable market).
Technological sovereignty: Strategic for the EU (production in France). Political and government support (potential subsidies).
