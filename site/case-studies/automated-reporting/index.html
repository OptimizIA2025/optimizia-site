Case study · Reporting & Dashboards

Market analysis and trading assistance, from raw data to a distributed verdict, with no manual work.

Designed and built in-house, this financial asset analysis system supports trading decisions on crypto markets. Above all it demonstrates orchestration expertise: a complete chain that collects market data (quotes, structural indicators, on-chain signals, fundamentals), has several specialized AI agents reason over it, settles on a directional recommendation, lays out the report and distributes it, with no manual step between raw data and delivered decision.

01 The pipeline, end to end

Five steps orchestrated in a chain. Each produces a clean deliverable consumed by the next. No human handover, no manual export.

01

Collection

Multi-source ingestion: quotes, structural indicators, on-chain signals, fundamentals.

02

AI analysis

A suite of AI agents scores each asset on five independent dimensions.

03

Verdict

Weighted synthesis into a global score and a directional verdict.

04

Reports

Automatic generation of an interactive HTML report and a printable PDF version.

05

Delivery

A card published to a Discord channel and indexed in the centralized real-time dashboard.

Collection
Data sources are polled on a scheduled trigger or on demand. Nothing is typed by hand: market feeds, structural indicators and complementary signals arrive in one normalized format, dated and versioned by analysis week.
Analysis
A suite of specialized AI agents processes each asset, pillar by pillar (project, traction, filters, fundamentals, technicals). Each agent produces a reasoned score and a short, verifiable justification.
Verdict
The five scores are weighted into a global score out of 100, turned into an actionable directional verdict. The decision engine is transparent: the report shows the detail that leads to the verdict.
Reports
Two deliverables are generated simultaneously: an interactive HTML report (overview, fundamentals, technicals, trade plan) and a PDF version for archiving and offline sharing. Layout, charts and radar are produced automatically.
Delivery
A summary card is published to a Discord channel with verdict, score, type and links to the reports. In parallel the analysis is indexed in a real-time dashboard with filters (type, verdict, week), search and a radar profile per asset.
