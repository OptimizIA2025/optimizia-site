01 Our conviction: start from the identity, not a template

A good website does not trace over a ready-made model. It starts from what makes the client singular, then dresses it with today's visual and technical codes.

Identity first
We look for inspiration where it already lives. For Audrey, the art direction was born from her own business card: her idea, surprising but excellent, set the tone for the whole site. The result reflects how she supports companies, with pragmatism, simplicity and closeness.
2026 trends
We stick to the year's webdesign trends and to accessibility and performance standards. No gratuitous fashion effects: every choice serves readability and conversion.
Native SEO / GEO
Every site is designed responsive and GEO-ready from its very first launch. Our process produces the site in one shot, already optimized for search engines and generative engines. Essential in 2026.
