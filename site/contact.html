FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the call work?

We meet for 20 minutes over video (Google Meet, Zoom or Teams, your choice). We ask a few questions about your context and current challenges, and share initial ideas for improvement. It's an informal, no-commitment conversation.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Not necessarily. If you have a precise issue or documents to share, that's a plus. The key thing is to come with your questions and your current challenges.

Is there any commitment after this call?

None at all. This discovery call is free and with no strings attached. If we identify collaboration opportunities together, we'll suggest a concrete next step.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, absolutely. You'll receive a confirmation email with a link to easily cancel or reschedule if your plans change.

Do you operate everywhere in France?

Our registered office is in Béziers (Hérault). We operate on-site across Occitanie and remotely across France and internationally. Video conferencing has been our norm for years, it suits this type of conversation perfectly.
