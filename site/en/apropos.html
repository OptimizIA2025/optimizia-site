Who we are

OptimizIA.xyz, AI at work in the field

OptimizIA.xyz is a network of experts founded by practitioners, not theorists, who believe AI only has value when it produces measurable results inside your organisation, with no unnecessary disruption and no jargon. We step in where it actually hurts: the processes that are slowing your growth.

Vision

Make AI accessible and sustainable for SMEs

We believe every SME leader should have access to the same performance levers as large groups, without the cost, risk and complexity that usually come with them. Our mission: support transformation through small, concrete steps, from diagnosis to industrial deployment, with a relentless focus on ROI.
