01 The pipeline, from source to prospect

Five steps orchestrated in a chain, triggered every morning. Each produces a clean deliverable consumed by the next. No human handover, no copy-paste.

01

Collection

25 sector feeds gathered every morning, with the full text of each article retrieved.

02

AI analysis

Every article is read by a language model that keeps what's actionable and drops the off-topic.

03

Prioritisation

Each retained signal is graded Tier 1, 2 or 3 against four business relevance criteria.

04

Brand ledger

Every brand is tallied run after run: repetition surfaces the hot prospect, invisible article by article.

05

Delivery

Table filled row by row and a daily briefing sent by email, even on days with no signal.

Collection
Twenty-five sector sources (trade press and watch queries) are polled on a scheduled trigger. Nothing is entered by hand: articles arrive in a normalised, dated format, and the full text is retrieved page by page, not just the headline.
Analysis
Articles are processed in batches by a language model, letting it compare sources against each other and deduplicate a single campaign cited by several outlets. Each retained signal comes with a short, verifiable rationale.
Prioritisation
The safeguard against noise isn't a tight filter, it's the tier. The system keeps things broad and ranks them: the user sees their Tier 1 first, without ever risking the loss of a weak but real signal.
Ledger
A dedicated tab holds one row per brand, accrued over time (occurrences, first and last sighting, best tier). A brand seen five times in three weeks in a target sector is a prospect, even if no single article shouted it.
Delivery
A briefing goes out every day, even at zero signal, to remove any ambiguity between "nothing to report" and "outage". A separate alert fires if a feed dies or the collection runs empty.

02 What keeps the watch reliable over time

The real risk of an automated watch isn't the visible crash, it's silent decay: sources dying one by one while the system still shows "all good". The chain is built to make that impossible.

Fail-safe
Three guardrails raise an explicit error if no source responds, if content can no longer be read, or if nothing enters the day's window, the scenario of a run that "succeeds" while writing nothing. Every failure sends a readable diagnosis by email.
Zero duplicates
Deduplication is twofold: within a run (two sources, one article) and between runs (already-known entries are never rewritten). A source that goes down is logged and skipped, it never breaks the collection.
Controlled cost
A single daily pass, in optimised batches: a few euros of processing a month to cover an entire press beat, where an equivalent human watch would mean several hours every day.

05 The gift: your Prompt Engineering expert

No need to be a pro. Copy the prompt below, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and you get an assistant that turns your fuzzy requests into clear, effective prompts, and tells you why. One of the reflexes shared during the workshop.

Prompt Engineering expert 
You are an expert in prompt engineering, specialised in large language models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). Your mission: turn a fuzzy request into a clear, structured and effective prompt, then briefly explain why.

# Your method
When I submit a task or an existing prompt, apply these techniques in order, keeping only the ones that help:
1. Clarity and direction: state the use context, the target audience and the expected format. Say what to do, not only what to avoid.
2. Examples (multishot): if consistency matters, add 2 or 3 examples of input and ideal output.
3. Step-by-step reasoning: for complex tasks, ask the model to think before answering.
4. Structure: separate the role, context, examples, instructions and data with named tags (<context>, <instructions>, <data>).
5. Role: give the model a precise persona, such as "You are an expert in [field] with X years of experience".
6. Output format: explicitly define the structure, length and format of the answer.

# How you respond
1. If my request is ambiguous, first ask 1 to 3 clarifying questions.
2. Deliver the improved prompt, ready to copy, in a structured block.
3. Add a short "Why" section: 3 to 5 bullets explaining the key choices.
4. Offer a shorter version if the task is simple.

# Your principles
- A prompt a colleague with no context would not understand, the model will not either.
- Always show an example of the ideal output rather than hoping for it.
- Prefer positive, precise instructions over vague prohibitions.
- Never make things up: if information is missing, ask for it.

Leaderboard

Frequently asked questions

Where do you operate?

+

We operate on-site in Occitanie and remotely across France and internationally. Most of our engagements involve teams spread across multiple sites, sometimes across several countries. Video conferencing and modern collaboration tools have become powerful, unavoidable enablers. We travel on-site when needed, this hybrid format suits our clients and has been our norm since 2001.

What's the difference between ginoux.xyz, romainben.cloud and OptimizIA.xyz?

+

Ginoux.xyz and romainben.cloud are our respective personal portfolios, our individual approach and way of working. OptimizIA.xyz is the agency Rémy Ginoux and Romain Ben co-founded. It's the brand under which we deliver AI & automation engagements, backed by a network of expert talent mobilised on demand depending on the project (dev, data, back-end / front-end architecture, design, automation). In short: we run the diagnostic phase to make sure the foundations are solid, then deploy our team on AI and automation integration.

Where do we start if we don't yet know whether AI is relevant for us?

+

That's exactly the situation we built Modules 1 and 2 for. In a few weeks, you get a clear, costed view of the most profitable use cases for your organisation, with no commitment on what comes next. You can then decide to keep going with a PoC to validate the concept (Module 3), or if you want our support to drive your transformation project, we'll provide a tailored quote (Serenity option).

We're an SME with under 40 employees, is this for us?

+

Yes, absolutely. One of the strengths of this new AI ecosystem is that it serves individuals just as well as small organisations. Small businesses and SMEs of up to 250 people are among our favourite clients, often able to decide and produce results quickly. The challenges are often the same as in large groups, but the gains come faster, with less political and decision-making complexity, and greater agility. It's a real strength!

How much does an engagement cost?

+

No two projects look alike, except in their ambition to lift performance. The estimate depends on your wishes, your objectives and how you choose to move forward. Our offer can be consumed à la carte using the modules that match your project maturity, or, depending on your ambition, you can pick the Serenity Menu: we support transformation projects end-to-end, on the consulting and project-piloting side as well as the dev side. Book a call with us for a first no-commitment conversation: it's the best way for us to grasp your challenge and give you a first set of answers within a few days.

Which tools do you use for automations?

+

It entirely depends on the use case, your IT environment and your objective: N8N, Make, Zapier for orchestration; Claude and Claude Code, other multi-agent tools, Grafana, Power BI for visualisation. We always recommend solutions your teams can maintain on their own, with no proprietary lock-in. Bear in mind that what's true today may already be obsolete in a month, that's why we run a continuous tech watch to propose the best options for your situation.

How do you handle data confidentiality and GDPR?

+

We systematically favour hosting on your existing infrastructure, so your data stays under your control. For clients who prefer to outsource hosting and maintenance, we run VPS servers located in France. The tools we deploy (self-hosted n8n, Claude API, and others) can be configured to meet your GDPR and data sovereignty requirements. No client data passes through our servers without your explicit agreement.
