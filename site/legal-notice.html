6. Cookies & trackers

This site runs two measurement tools, independent of each other, only one of which depends on your agreement. The first is an audience measurement tool developed in-house, which does not rely on any third party (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising service). It runs without a consent banner, because it meets the four exemption conditions set by the French data protection authority (CNIL): purpose strictly limited to audience measurement, aggregated results, no third-party recipient, no tracking across websites.

What is collected

  • Page visited, title, timestamp
  • Navigation events: CTA clicks, external links, internal navigation
  • Scroll depth (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)
  • Time spent on page
  • Traffic source category (e.g. "google", "linkedin", "direct"), never the exact referrer URL

What is not collected

  • Name, email address, IP address
  • Precise geolocation data
  • Browsing history outside this site

Technical identifiers

Two random UUID identifiers are stored in your browser:

  • oia_vid (localStorage): visit identifier, kept for 13 months at most. It carries its creation date; beyond that it is erased and regenerated, with no link to the previous one.
  • oia_sid (sessionStorage): session identifier, deleted when the tab is closed

These identifiers do not make you anonymous but pseudonymous: they do not say who you are, they tell one visit apart from another. The associated events are kept for 25 months.

Infrastructure & transfers

Data is sent to our own n8n instance hosted in Europe. No data is shared with advertising platforms or third parties.

For organic SEO performance monitoring, we consult Google Search Console via its API. This data (clicks, impressions, positions) consists of aggregates already processed by Google and is not linked to individual visitor behaviour.

Objecting to audience measurement

Since this measurement is exempt, it is not preceded by any banner, but you can object at any time: add ?notrack=1 to the address of any page on this site and it is durably disabled in your browser. You may also write to contact@optimizia.xyz.

Session replay: Microsoft Clarity

The second tool is Microsoft Clarity, which replays browsing sessions and produces heatmaps, so we can understand where visitors get stuck. Session replay does not fall under the regime of aggregated audience measurement: no exemption applies to it, so it is subject to your consent.

A banner is shown on your first visit. Until you accept, nothing is loaded and no cookie is set. After acceptance, two first-party cookies are set, and only those two: _clck and _clsk. The advertising signal is passed as denied, so no Microsoft advertising cookie is set. You can change your mind at any time through the “Manage cookies” link in the footer of every page; declining erases the cookies already set and reloads the page to stop collection in progress.

The service is provided by Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited and hosted on Azure. Transfers to Microsoft Corporation outside the European Union are governed by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Retention is tiered: recordings are kept for 30 days, then a residual sample (1% of recordings or 10 per day, whichever is greater), flagged sessions and heatmaps are kept for up to 9 months. The content of form fields is masked at source by Clarity, and that masking cannot be turned off.
