How an engagement runs

Four steps, and at each one you decide whether to go further.

  1. 01

    A 20-minute call

    Free and without a sales pitch: you describe your situation, we tell you honestly whether AI can help.

  2. 02

    A written proposal

    Scope, deliverables, timeline and a fixed price, in writing, before any work starts.

  3. 03

    A narrow scope first

    We start small so you can check the value before anything grows. For a PoC, 13 days on average to a working MVP.

  4. 04

    Handover or copiloting

    Documentation and training so your team runs it on its own. If you prefer, we stay on to maintain and extend it.

Questions about pricing

Why “from” rather than a set price?

Because the price depends on your scope: how many processes, how many tools to connect, how much data. After the first call you get a fixed quote, before any work starts.

Do we have to start with the diagnostic?

No. If you already know which process you want to automate, we can go straight to a PoC. The diagnostic is for when you don't yet know where AI will pay off.

Can we stop after one step?

Yes. Each format stands on its own, and the diagnostic deliverable stays usable even if you implement it without us.

What comes on top of the price?

Software subscriptions and API usage, paid directly to the providers. For an SME workload they usually come to a few tens of euros per month, and we list them in the proposal.
